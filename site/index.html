HTML has gotten better. It still doesn’t have tabs.

MannerHTML adds the behavior, not the component. Author the HTML you already own. Keep the content, IDs, URLs, and CSS. Get accessible tabs without a framework or a rendering runtime.

The important part is what stays.

MannerHTML never replaces your markup. It normalizes the relationships and owns the interaction lifecycle.

  • ordinary fragment links before JS
  • roving focus after upgrade
  • no required library CSS
  • no URL or history surprises
